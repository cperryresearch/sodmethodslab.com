Development-stage methods lab

SOD Methods Lab LLC

Structured motion-analysis methods, audit interfaces, and technical documentation for restraint-first review of trajectory and state-segmented motion data.

The lab emphasizes traceability, bounded interpretation, and clear separation between observed motion structure and unsupported conclusions.

Lab posture

SOD Methods Lab LLC houses the SOD theoretical framework and develops applied review tools such as PC-MAW, an in-progress audit-interface workbench for structured motion review.

The work is documentation-centered and restraint-first: public materials are intended to support reproducible review, careful vocabulary, and transparent separation between measurement, interpretation, and unsupported claims.

Current focus

  • Structured Observation Dynamics (SOD) framework documentation
  • PC-MAW audit-interface and motion-review workbench development
  • Trajectory and state-segmented motion review methods
  • Geometry-first motion analysis documentation
  • Reproducible review tools and technical writing
  • Bounded candidate-support summaries

Public / open work

Public-facing development materials, documentation, and review tools may be made available through GitHub and related documentation channels.

GitHub: github.com/cperryresearch

Boundary of claims

SOD Methods Lab LLC does not provide regulatory certification, official aerospace safety determinations, object identification, origin claims, or propulsion claims.

The lab’s work is focused on motion structure, traceability, review discipline, technical documentation, and bounded interpretation.

Private / applied review work

Applied review workflows, internal evaluation criteria, candidate-support packaging, cohort-specific strategy, and decision or emission layers are maintained privately.

Appropriate research, documentation, or applied review inquiries may be discussed through direct contact.
